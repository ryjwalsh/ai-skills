## At a glance
Tour of the IronYun/Vaidio Partner Portal (branded `IRONYUN PARTNER PORTAL`): where partners get datasheets, documents, videos and presentations, run the four sizing calculators, download user guides and open department-routed support tickets, register new deals, read the FAQ and reach the shared demo site. Vaidio University links to it through the `Partner Portal` link in the university header; the portal is hosted under www.vaidio.ai. [ST2:resource-library] [ST2:deal-registration] [ST2:demo-site]

## Resource Library  [ST2:resource-library]
- Holds all datasheets, videos, documents and presentations for getting the most out of Vaidio.
- Three ways to narrow a search: Resource Type (datasheet, presentation, etc.), Category, Analytic.
- Three how-to videos, one under each heading: "How to search based on resource type", "How to search based on Category", "How to perform a full search" (no further lesson text).
- Filter bar, left to right: `Resource type` dropdown, `Category` dropdown, `Analytics` dropdown, `Search...` free-text box, magnifier search button, `Reset filters` link.
- Results render as cards with a type badge (e.g. `VIDEO`), title, description and a `VIEW` button. Example resources: "AI Friday Forum | 8.0 Release | 03/08/2024" (recording of the 8.0 software release and GenAI discussion held March 8, 2024); "Scene Change Demo - Spilled Coffee Detection"; "Scene Change Demo - Spill / Leak Detection".
- The dropdown option values are not captured (only shown inside the videos).

## Support  [ST2:support]
Support section = product documentation + ticket submission to specific Vaidio departments.

User Guides sub-section:
- Topics: setting up Vaidio, operational procedures, deployment strategies, Vaidio integrations, and more.
- A separate user guide exists for each analytic engine, with instructions specific to that analytic.
- Format: downloadable `.pdf`.

Support (ticket) sub-section — the `support` tab is the recommended channel to reach Vaidio staff; route by department:

| Need | Contact |
|---|---|
| Software, hardware, configuration questions/requests | Tech Support |
| Sales documentation, sales assistance/support | Sales Support (or your Vaidio sales representative directly) |
| Marketing questions, material requests, guidance | Marketing Team |

## Deal registration  [ST2:deal-registration]
- Purpose: partners register new client projects under the `Deal Registration` tab (`New Deal Registration` in the portal nav). Submitting the form prompts a Vaidio team member to reach out about the request — it is the mechanism for getting Vaidio's attention and support on a new project.
- How-to video: "How to register a new project".
- Form page title: "New Deal Registration — Register your Vaidio project using the form below."
- First fields, all required (marked *): `CONTACT NAME`, `WORK EMAIL`, `END USER COMPANY NAME`, `ARE YOU WORKING WITH A DISTRIBUTOR?` (dropdown, default `Please Select`). Further fields exist below the fold and were not captured. [partial]

## Demo site  [ST2:demo-site]
1. Log in to the Partner Portal and open the `Vaidio Demo Site` page.
2. Log in to the demo site with the shared partner demo account — shared partner demo credentials are published in the lesson; not reproduced here.
- The demo site has public camera streams available for testing before a partner sets up its own account with its own camera streams.
- For how to actually run a Vaidio demo, the lesson points to the in-depth "Vaidio Setup Tutorial" course (TT2 Vaidio Setup Detailed Tutorial).
