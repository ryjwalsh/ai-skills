## Four keys to effective prospecting  [ST3:four-keys-to-effective-prospecting]
Section 1 agenda, at the highest level: keys to effective prospecting; how Vaidio and video cameras work together; good opportunities for Vaidio in general; less-ideal opportunities in general. [ST3:start-here-a-camerabased-solution]

The four keys that make opportunity identification efficient and accelerate the prospect's sales journey:
1. Know the high-level context for Vision AI and the Vaidio platform.
2. Understand how Vaidio and video cameras work together.
3. Understand the key problems and pain points Vaidio addresses.
4. Understand key verticals and Vision AI applications.

## A camera-based solution  [ST3:vaidio--vision-ai-is-a-camerabased-solution]
- Metaphor: cameras = the "eyes"; Vaidio, the AI Vision software analysing the camera video = the "brain".
- First big value point: Vaidio adds intelligence to EXISTING cameras — the customer spends less (no new cameras) and gets more (intelligence, accuracy, functionality). Almost every public and private organisation already has cameras.
- New cameras are supported when the customer's goals require them, but the core differentiator is adding intelligence to existing cameras and integrating with existing video management systems (VMS).
- Customer value framing: extends the useful life of existing infrastructure and improves the ROI on it.

## Three value vectors  [ST3:vaidio--realtime-search-and-data-applications]
Every video-analytics context/problem has three "vectors" (functional value drivers); usually ONE is the primary value driver for a given prospect. Vaidio delivers all three with EVERY video-analytic application, not one per product.

| # | Vector | Qualifying example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real-time monitoring, detection and alerting | A bank needing faster real-time intrusion detection |
| 2 | Investigative / forensic video search | A sprawling corporate campus needing shorter incident-investigation time |
| 3 | Business data and intelligence gathering | A retail store wanting video-derived data for revenue optimisation |

## Camera-count sizing  [ST3:initial-sizing-the-number-of-cameras]
- First sizing input for any opportunity = the number of cameras that need Vaidio analytics. It gives (a) the general boundaries of software (licence) cost and (b) a rough idea of compute/hardware requirements → a rough potential deal size.
- Heuristic: bigger organisation → more cameras.
- Caution: not every camera needs advanced Vaidio analytics. Each additional analytic increases deal scope. Total camera count matters, but the number of cameras that NEED Vaidio intelligence matters more for initial deal sizing.

## Qualification profile
| | Ideal opportunity [ST3:ideal-opportunities] | Less suitable opportunity [ST3:less-suitable-opportunities] |
|---|---|---|
| Camera count | More than 100 cameras | Fewer than 10 cameras |
| Infrastructure | Wants to extend the useful life of existing cameras / camera infrastructure | — |
| Footprint | Cameras distributed across multiple buildings, sites or geographies | — |
| Applications | Multiple uses/applications for AI video analytics; security, safety and surveillance applications AND/OR vertical- or application-specific Vision AI use cases | Needs only basic security (motion detection, basic intrusion) with limited or no video analytics |
| Commercials | — | Price is the main decision factor; limited budget |

Why the ideal profile fits [ST3:what-makes-these-opportunities-ideal]: Vaidio is enterprise-grade software for multiple, sophisticated, demanding applications at scale; the sweet spot is customers with 100+ cameras and multiple Vision AI applications. Proof points to quote:
- Open platform — works with any IP camera.
- Adds intelligence to both new and existing cameras.
- Scales from 10s to 1,000s of cameras.
- Proven accurate on tens of thousands of real-world cameras.
- Comprehensive platform: dozens of discrete analytics functions out of the box.

VaaS routing rule [ST3:less-suitable-opportunities]: small-camera-count, low-budget, few-analytics prospects are better served by providers offering "Vaidio as a Service", who can aggregate many small customers — refer them to a VaaS/MSP partner rather than selling a direct deployment.

## Verticals and use cases

### Education  [ST3:vertical-application-examples--education]
- Scope: K-12 schools, colleges, universities.
- Gun violence is top of mind, but IronYun's position is to address school safety holistically: a single-point weapon-detection product improves safety less than a platform that raises the whole campus security posture.
- Weapon detection is called "Specialized Object" in Vaidio; it becomes far more valuable once the other vectors are covered — sell the platform, add weapon detection on top.
- Education solution points (10):
  1. Perimeter security
  2. Face-based access control
  3. License-plate-based access control
  4. Crowding
  5. Loitering
  6. Occupancy
  7. PPE and masks
  8. Smoke and fire
  9. Vandalism
  10. Vaping

### Retail  [ST3:vertical-application-examples--retail]
- Value themes: loss prevention; inventory management and warehouse safety; video-driven merchandising / revenue optimisation. Retail is strong on ALL three vectors (real-time, video search, BI).
- Retail applications (9):
  1. Loss prevention enhancement
  2. Self-checkout monitoring
  3. Shoplift detection
  4. Theft investigation
  5. Inventory, stock, shelf, and warehouse monitoring
  6. Shopper analytics and store data
  7. Hot and cold zones, dwell points
  8. People counting
  9. Merchandizing and revenue optimization

### Smart Cities  [ST3:vertical-application-examples--smart-cities]
- Value themes: traffic flow, traffic management, traffic safety.
- Budget differentiator: Vaidio turns ANY camera into an LPR camera (competitors need expensive purpose-built LPR cameras).
- Law-enforcement angle: LPR for stolen-vehicle detection; recovering stolen cars tends to reduce associated crimes and overall crime rates.
- Smart-city applications (10):
  1. Facility security
  2. Access control
  3. Traffic analysis
  4. Traffic light monitoring
  5. Intersection monitoring
  6. Speed monitoring
  7. Automated vehicle ticketing
  8. LPR for stolen vehicles
  9. Smoke and fire
  10. Parking management

### Healthcare  [ST3:vertical-application-examples--healthcare]
- Value themes: visitor management; equipment tracking; parking; safer and more efficient shipping and receiving; patient out-of-bed detection to reduce falls and the associated litigation/liability burden.
- Healthcare applications (10):
  1. Intrusion detection
  2. Face-based access control
  3. LPR-based access control
  4. Visitor check-in
  5. Patient monitoring
  6. Person fall
  7. Person out-of-bed
  8. Equipment tracking
  9. Shipping and receiving
  10. Parking management

### Logistics, Shipping, Trucking  [ST3:logistics-shipping-trucking]
- Value themes: manage and secure goods across the supply chain; protect own property, vehicles and people.
- High-ROI example — truck yards: GPS/geo-tracking is not granular enough to track trucks and containers in a yard; loading, unloading, idling and parking time is money; embedding RF trackers in yard asphalt costs hundreds of thousands to millions per yard; a few cameras with Vaidio track more items and produce more data than any competing solution; across hundreds or thousands of yards the savings and data value can reach tens of millions.
- Logistics applications (6):
  1. Container tracking
  2. Forklift proximity
  3. IMO ship identifier tracking
  4. Inventory tracking
  5. Truck yard monitoring
  6. Warehouse safety
- The 8.1 `Container ID` analytic serves container tracking (release-8.1.md).

## Clarifying the need and the value  [ST3:clarifying-the-need-and-the-value]
Qualification foundation = four things: the vertical; the number of cameras needing intelligent video analytics; the potential Vaidio applications; the ways Vaidio solves problems / drives value. With these a seller can identify opportunities, estimate deal size, and explore objectives and value-creation points with the prospect.
