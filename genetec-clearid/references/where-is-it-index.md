# Where is it - keyword to breadcrumb index

Alphabetical lookup from a control name, feature name or piece of jargon straight to its location in the ClearID web portal. Prefixes `AM` = **Access Management**, `MW` = **My workspace**. Placeholders in square brackets are records the user picks. [S2]

Use this file first for any "where do I..." question. If the term is not here, fall back to `navigation-map.md`, and if the term is conceptual rather than a control, go to `concepts-and-workflows.md`.

## Disambiguation notes

Several concepts exist in more than one place. Always say which one governs what.

- **Approval workflow** is configured per area (**Areas > [area] > Access request configurations**) for area access, per role (**Roles > [role] > General**) for role membership, and per visit profile (**Sites > [site] > Visitor management > [profile] > Planned visits**) for visits, with an extra per-visitor workflow on the area (**Areas > [area] > Visitor management**).
- **Notifications** exist at three levels: account-wide on/off and opt-out rules (**Administration > Notifications**), per-site content and language (**Sites > [site] > Notifications**), and per-user opt-ins (**My Profile > Preferences**).
- **Theme** exists twice: the account brand (**Administration > Account configuration > Branding**), the kiosk look (**Sites > [site] > Visitor management > Kiosk theme**) and the individual light/dark choice (**My Profile > Preferences > Theme mode**).
- **Documents** exist twice: site-level access request documents (**Sites > [site] > Access configurations**) and area-level, where the area chooses to include or exclude the site documents (**Areas > [area] > Access request configurations**).
- **Access** appears as a report (**Reports > Access requests**), as a per-identity list (**Identities > [person] > Access**), as a per-area list (**Areas > [area] > Access**) and as a per-role list (**Roles > [role] > Access**).
- **Credentials** appear as a sync setting (**Administration > Credentials**), as a per-system format list (**Administration > Systems > [system]**), as requestable types on an area (**Areas > [area] > Access request configurations**) and as a per-person list (**Identities > [person] > Credentials**).
