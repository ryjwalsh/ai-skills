## How the two kinds of source differ

**S2 is the authority for navigation.** Routes, tab names, tab order, sub-tabs, settings-card names and field labels come from the live portal, because the documentation does not publish a complete route map.

**S1 and S3-S15 are the authority for behaviour.** Why a feature exists, what a workflow does, who receives which e-mail, which hardware is certified, and which ports must be open come from the documentation.

Where the two disagree, prefer the portal capture for *where* and the documentation for *what* and *why*, and tell the user that a label may have shifted since 2026-07-29.

## Scope boundary

The following were deliberately **not** treated as sources, so do not attribute claims to them: the Genetec developer portal and API reference, Security Center (non-SaaS) documentation, the Security Center SaaS Setup / Pre-Deployment / User guides, Federation in Security Center SaaS, and Genetec Cloudlink documentation. They are linked as related content from the ClearID publication and are the right next stop for platform-level questions, but nothing in this skill is sourced from them.
